Our Story

South Hills Church of Christ is a community pulling together to love God, to love His church, and to love the people He has created.

We are a Bible-based congregation in the hills of Helena, Montana, seeking to follow Jesus together. What makes our story unique? We started as three separate church families who chose to become one.

Three Families, One Church

After years of partnering on youth missions and Vacation Bible School, three Helena congregations realized they were stronger united.

Rocky Mountain Church of Christ

Helena Church of Christ

Big Sky Church of Christ

South Hills Church of Christ

United in 2011

That same spirit of unity drives everything we do today — worshiping together, serving our neighbors, and welcoming anyone who walks through our doors.

"Make every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace."

Ephesians 4:3

Our Mission

We are focused on three things that shape everything we do.

Revealing God

Sharing God's love and truth with our community through worship, teaching, and the way we live our daily lives.

Renewing Lives

Walking alongside people as they grow in faith, find healing, and discover the purpose God has for their lives.

Rejoicing Together

Celebrating God's goodness through worship, fellowship, and the deep joy that comes from life in community.
