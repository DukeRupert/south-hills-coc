South Hills Church of Christ is an independent, non-denominational congregation. We identify as a "back to the Bible" church, seeking unity by following Scripture as our guide for faith and practice.

God

We believe that God is one God, existing in three persons (Father, Son, and Holy Spirit), who created all things and made them "very good" for relationship with Him (Genesis 1:31).

The Bible

We affirm the Scriptures as God's inspired and authoritative word (2 Timothy 3:16-17, 2 Peter 1:20-21). We believe the Bible directs us to Jesus and demonstrates how God desires us to live.

Our Approach

Rather than following creeds or traditions, we seek to understand and apply the teachings of Scripture in our daily lives. We believe this approach promotes unity among believers and honors God's design for His church.

Compass Classes

For those exploring Christian faith or wanting to learn more about what we believe, we offer Compass Classes - introductory courses covering the foundations of Christianity and the Christian life.
