Your First Visit

We know visiting a new church can feel a little uncertain. Here's what you can expect so there are no surprises.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. You'll see everything from jeans to slacks. There's no dress code — just come comfortable.

How long is the service?

Sunday worship is about one hour. Bible class before worship is about 45 minutes.

Will I be singled out?

No. We're glad you're here, but we won't put you on the spot. Feel free to participate at your own pace.

What about parking?

We have plenty of free parking right at the building. You won't have trouble finding a spot.

What to Expect

Here's what a typical Sunday morning looks like at South Hills.

A Cappella Singing

We sing spiritual songs together without instrumental accompaniment, with lyrics displayed on screens for everyone to follow.

Lord's Supper

Following a brief message about Jesus, unleavened bread and grape juice are distributed. This is a time of reflection on Christ's sacrifice.

Sermon

A 20-30 minute Bible-based message from our minister, focused on how Scripture applies to our daily lives.

Fellowship

After the service, we invite you to stay and connect with our church family. We love meeting new faces!

Families Welcome

We love children at South Hills! Kids are always welcome in our worship services. We also offer age-appropriate children's church after communion for those who prefer it.

Your family's safety is important to us. We maintain security measures during services with attention to child and family protection.

Next Steps

If you're exploring Christian faith or want to learn more about our church, we offer Compass Classes - introductory courses covering the foundations of Christianity and what it means to follow Jesus.
