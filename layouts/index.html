Who We Are

A community pulling together to love God, love His church, and love the people He has created.

Discipleship

Growing together through Bible study, small groups, and classes that equip us to follow Jesus more faithfully every day.

Fellowship

Building genuine relationships through shared meals, service projects, and community events that make this family feel like home.

Worship

Gathering each Sunday to sing, pray, remember Christ in communion, and hear God's Word taught with practical application.

What to Expect

Our Sunday gatherings incorporate singing, prayer, communion participation, and Bible-focused sermons.

We sing spiritual songs together without instrumental accompaniment, with lyrics displayed on screens.
