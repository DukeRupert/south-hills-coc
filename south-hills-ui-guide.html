Color Usage Rules
Rust Detail only. Logo mark, ghost button border, text links, location pill, card listen links. Never use as a background field larger than a pill or badge.
Sage Accent + action. Eyebrow lines, series tags, sage CTA button, left-border on info cards. The primary "look here" color — keep it purposeful.
Sage Dark Structure. Nav bar, sidebar panel, primary button background, H1/H2 color. The heaviest visual weight on the page.
Stone Surface. Page background (#F4F0EB), sermon cards and info panels (#EAE5DE). Keeps everything warm without competing with any accent color.
